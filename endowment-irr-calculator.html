Important notes

  • Internal Rate of Return (IRR) is the annualised rate of return implied by the premiums you pay and the benefits you receive, taking into account when each payment is made or received. It represents the annual interest rate at which an investment would need to grow to match the same cash flows over the same period. This is a calculated measure of return, not a return guaranteed or declared by the insurer.
  • Bonuses and other non-guaranteed amounts shown in a benefit illustration may turn out lower or higher than illustrated.
  • The same premium is assumed every year, paid at the start of each policy year, with benefits received at the end of the year. Results may be approximate for older policies where the premium changed over time, for example because GST applied in earlier years.
  • Disclaimer. This calculator provides illustrative estimates based on the information entered. Actual benefits, bonuses, tax treatment and returns may differ. The calculation does not assign a monetary value to the life cover provided by the policy. It is intended for educational purposes and should not be the sole basis for an insurance or investment decision. Please verify the figures against your policy documents and applicable tax rules.
