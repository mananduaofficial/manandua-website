Wealth Management

Goal-aligned portfolios across mutual funds and Specialized Investment Funds (SIFs), spanning equity, debt, and select alternative categories, structured around your risk profile, time horizon, and evolving priorities.

What this service covers

Wealth management here is built around outcomes, not products. Every investment recommendation begins with a clear understanding of what you're working towards, how long you have, and the level of risk you're comfortable taking, before any investment is considered.

  • Goal-based fund recommendations: mutual fund and SIF combinations recommended across equity, debt, and alternative categories, aligned to the specific requirements of each goal
  • Mutual Funds & Specialized Investment Funds (SIFs): access across both categories, so eligible investors can be positioned in the structure best suited to their goal and risk profile
  • SIP and lump sum strategy: a disciplined approach combining systematic investments with timely allocations where appropriate
  • Risk-aware recommendations: fund combinations recommended in line with your risk profile and time horizon, revisited as your goals move closer
  • Ongoing review: periodic reviews with fresh recommendations to help keep your investments aligned as markets and life evolve

Regulatory & disclosure

Mutual fund schemes are distributed as an AMFI-registered Mutual Fund Distributor under a valid ARN (details available in the Investor Charter). Specialized Investment Funds (SIFs) are made available to eligible investors in line with applicable SEBI norms. Investments are offered under the Regular Plan option. Commission is received from Asset Management Companies for distribution, in accordance with the AMFI Code of Conduct.

Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks. Please read all scheme-related documents carefully before investing.
